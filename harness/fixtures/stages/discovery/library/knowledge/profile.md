# Organizational Profile

## Who We Are

- Northwind Outfitters is an online retailer of outdoor clothing and camping gear.
- A team of twelve runs the store, the warehouse, and customer support.

## Where We're Going

- Ship every order within one business day.
- Grow repeat customers by a fifth over the next year.

## How We Plan to Get There

- Automate routine warehouse and order work so the team spends its time on customers.
- Keep the stack small and managed, using hosted services where they fit.

## What Guides Our Decisions

- Protect customer data before adding features.
- Prefer services the team already runs over new vendors.
