# Automate Weekly Order Restock Alerts

## Problem

Popular items sell out before anyone notices, and orders for them wait for stock, which breaks one-day shipping.

## Actors

The warehouse lead, who reorders stock, and the store manager, who approves purchase orders.

## Current Process

Every Monday the warehouse lead exports stock levels from the order service into a spreadsheet and checks each item against its reorder point by hand.

## Desired Change

Each week, items at or below their reorder point are found automatically and the warehouse lead receives one alert listing them.

## Success Measure

No order waits for restock on an item that was below its reorder point on the previous alert.
