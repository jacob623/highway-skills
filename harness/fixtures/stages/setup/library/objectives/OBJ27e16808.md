## Statement

Ship every customer order within one business day of payment.

## Success Measures

At least 95 percent of orders leave the warehouse within one business day, measured weekly.

## Rationale

The Profile names one-day shipping as where Northwind Outfitters is going, and late shipments are the most common support complaint.
