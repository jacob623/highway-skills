# Contract: Platform-standard entries

## When it applies

The standard's Technology is a Platform title in `library/coding-standards/codify-platforms.md`. Compare without regard to case. The whole Technology value matches. A language with no platform row does not match.

## `platform entries` in `highway-codify-config`

1. When to use is `[Platform] is used`.
2. When not to use is `[Platform] is not used`.
3. `[Platform]` is that row's title.
4. Do not follow `entry level`. Do not keep other saved entries.
5. Do not split these entries. Do not rewrite them.
6. Return to `use entries`.

## Proposal shape

```markdown
**When to use:**
1. [Platform] is used

   **Why it matters:** [One sentence stating the consequence of this entry.]

**When not to use:**
1. [Platform] is not used

   **Why it matters:** [One sentence stating the consequence of this entry.]
```

The saved cell keeps the entry only.

## Rejected

- A second When to use entry on an unedited platform-standard proposal.
- `entry level` classes on that proposal.
- The phrases on an unedited language-standard proposal.

A person's edit of either entry is kept. A remove shows the saved entries.
