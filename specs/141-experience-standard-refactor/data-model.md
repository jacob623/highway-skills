# Data Model: Experience Standard Runtime Contract Refactor

This feature introduces no persisted fields, schema changes, runtime state, or new storage. The entities below describe the transient and normative boundaries that the refactored document must retain.

## Experience Standard

- **Meaning:** The shared Layer 2 contract governing user-visible output and interaction across Highway skills.
- **Owner:** `.highway/governance/experience-standard.md`.
- **Validation:** Every current X-rule identifier and Observable remains addressable; retained guidance has one authoritative home.
- **Persistence:** None; owning skills determine artifact persistence at their declared acceptance boundaries.

## X-rule

- **Fields:** Stable identifier, normative obligation, user-visible Observable, and tier/context as applicable.
- **Validation:** IDs remain unchanged, and the preserved behavior remains materially equivalent except for removed historical or compatibility framing.
- **Relationship:** X-rules compose through the single Interaction Model rather than parallel prose loops.

## Working Idea

- **Contents:** User contributions, Highway contributions, interpretations, implications, alternatives, assumptions, concerns, and substantive responses.
- **State:** Transient, non-authoritative, and subject to contextual re-evaluation.
- **Transition:** It continues while substantive understanding improves and may reopen after a substantive Contribution Opportunity response.

## Converged Proposal

- **Meaning:** An owning-workflow-complete candidate whose substantive Working Idea has also converged.
- **Validation:** Domain completeness alone is insufficient; useful remaining substantive development prevents Converged Proposal presentation.
- **Transition:** It may proceed to the owning workflow's acceptance boundary.

## Artifact Acceptance Boundary

- **Meaning:** The owning workflow's declared decision point for promoting a complete candidate into accepted user-owned knowledge.
- **Persistence:** The owning workflow performs whatever persistence it declares; the Experience Standard does not redefine it.

## Interaction Model

- **Meaning:** The single compact explanatory loop showing how context reuse, contribution, re-evaluation, selective clarification, Contribution Opportunity, convergence, acceptance, and contextual follow-up combine.
- **Validation:** No second full prose representation of the adaptive collaboration loop remains.
