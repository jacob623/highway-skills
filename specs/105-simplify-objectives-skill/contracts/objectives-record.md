# Contract: Objective record

**Source**: `.highway/library/templates/output/objective-record.md`

**Version**: 1.0.0. This feature does not edit the file.

## Retained structure

The record remains:

- frontmatter with `id`, `title`, `status`, and `capabilities`
- `## Statement`
- `## Success Measures`
- `## Rationale`

## Absent field

Highway Relevance is not a heading, a frontmatter key, or any other stored field.

Significance is not a heading or a frontmatter key.

## Compatibility

A current 1.0.0 Objective record remains valid. This feature does not migrate records and does not change identifier, catalog, or allocation rules.
