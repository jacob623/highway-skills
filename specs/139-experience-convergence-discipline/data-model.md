# Data Model: Experience Convergence Discipline

This feature has no runtime data model. The entities below describe the governed interaction concepts that the Experience Standard must preserve and make distinguishable.

## Working Idea

- **Purpose**: Transient developing understanding before artifact acceptance.
- **Content**: User contributions, Highway contributions, interpretations, connections, implications, alternatives, tensions, assumptions, recommendations, and corrections.
- **Authority**: Non-authoritative until an owning workflow's existing acceptance boundary is crossed.
- **Lifecycle**: Begins from user or Highway contribution; is re-evaluated after substantive changes; may receive further contributions; settles before a Converged Proposal; is either accepted through the owner or remains transient.

## Converged Proposal

- **Purpose**: Complete candidate artifact or artifact set presented at the owner's acceptance boundary.
- **Preconditions**: The owning workflow has a complete candidate and the substantive Working Idea has converged enough that further collaborative development is no longer improving it.
- **Relationship**: Domain completeness is necessary for a candidate, but does not alone establish conversational convergence.
- **Authority**: Presentation does not itself accept or persist the candidate; existing owner acceptance semantics remain authoritative.

## Substantive Contribution

- **Purpose**: New reasoning material that can change active understanding or candidate substance.
- **Sources**: The person or Highway.
- **Examples**: Adding, changing, correcting, removing, distinguishing, qualifying, redirecting, combining, narrowing, challenging, or extending substantive content.
- **Lifecycle**: Re-evaluated with relevant Active Reasoning Context and accepted context before selecting the next behavior.

## Conversational Clarification

- **Purpose**: Resolve consequential uncertainty when user-owned information is required.
- **State**: Transient interaction behavior; it does not create a deterministic clarification record by itself.
- **Relationship**: Distinct from Contribution Opportunity and artifact acceptance. A clarification can reveal new development, but it does not automatically satisfy the Contribution Opportunity.

## Contribution Opportunity

- **Purpose**: Give the person a meaningful chance to add, correct, remove, or extend materially Highway-shaped Working Idea substance before convergence.
- **Lifecycle**: Occurs when applicable; a substantive response re-enters development; a response with no substantive addition may be followed by the Converged Proposal once the Working Idea has settled.
- **Constraint**: It is not a recurring ceremonial question or an acceptance boundary.

## Active Reasoning Context

- **Purpose**: Transient task-anchored context used to combine active contributions and relevant accepted context.
- **Content**: Developing ideas, unresolved questions, implications, alternatives, tensions, and connections.
- **Persistence**: No new file, persisted state, or exposed implementation identifier is introduced by this feature.

## Ownership Relationships

- The **Highway Identity** document supplies behavioral direction.
- The **Experience Standard** owns shared user-visible interaction obligations and explanatory guidance.
- The **owning workflow** determines domain completeness, candidate validity, acceptance, persistence, and readiness.
- The **Highway Skills Constitution** remains the governing Constitution boundary.
- `highway-clarify` remains the owner of deterministic clarification artifacts when explicitly invoked.
