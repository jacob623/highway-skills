# Retirements: Onboarding and Assessment Guidance

The static onboarding capability is renamed from Highway to AI-Assisted Engineering and Planning. These lines are removed from `highway-onboarding`:

- **Highway:** Connect accepted business goals, decisions, architecture, and implementation work into a single traceable system of knowledge.
- When the capability title is Highway and the session identifies the agent platform by name, that name is the provider.
- Omit it when an accepted ADR already records the Highway capability for the same agent-platform name.

`.highway/tools/tests/static-highway-capability.test.sh` required those three sentences. Those assertions are superseded by the AI-Assisted Engineering and Planning line, its provider rule, and its inclusion rule.
