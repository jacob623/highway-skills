# Contract: Batch Control and NFR review

## When it appears

Asking to review the selected Controls, or the selected NFRs, opens this review. Opening stored active Controls, or stored active NFRs, uses the same card. It replaces the one-at-a-time capture.

## Controls message

**Here's what I've captured as your controls:**

**[Title]:** [Statement]

**Why it matters:** [Rationale]

---

## NFRs message

**Here's what I've captured as your NFRs:**

**[Title]:** [Statement]

**Why it matters:** [Rationale]

---

The rule appears between items only. The statement is one sentence. A Control sentence says what must remain true. An NFR sentence says how a solution must behave and includes a measurable bound only when accepted evidence supports it.

**What would you add, correct, or remove?**

If this is accurate, just say so.

## Replies

| Reply | Result |
|---|---|
| Accurate, including "looks good" | Store every item still in the set. Show no stored record and no closing line. |
| Add, correct, or remove | Show the whole revised set again. Store nothing yet. |
| Question or unclear | Show the same set again. |
| Decline the set | Store none of that set. |

The removed lines are:

- **Here's what I've captured as your Control:**
- **Here's what I've captured as your NFR:**
- Your accepted Controls now cover [short phrases for the held Controls].
- Your accepted NFRs now cover [short phrases for the held NFRs].
- Next we'll develop **[next title]**.

The review does not include **Success looks like:**.

## Stored text

| Record section | Review text |
|---|---|
| Statement | The statement beside the title |
| Rationale | The line beside **Why it matters:** |
