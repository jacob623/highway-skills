# Feature 156 Data Model

This feature introduces no persisted data, schema, or state transition. The following conceptual entities describe the document contract only.

## Accepted Profile Evidence

Profile material accepted by the person and available to ground advisory reasoning.

**Constraint**: Every structure and every later chain step must be traceable to accepted evidence.

## Structure-Revealing Contribution

An existing contribution type that makes a supported relationship, role, pattern, hierarchy, decision criterion, tension, dependency, or organizing principle explicit without merely summarizing the evidence.

**Constraint**: It remains a Working Idea and is not a candidate or accepted Profile content.

## Connected Advisory Chain

One linear advisory move composed of a revealed structure followed by zero or more directly derived steps, with the supported forms limited to a connected implication, possibility, or tradeoff chain.

**Constraint**: Each step derives from the immediately preceding step. The chain cannot branch into independent alternatives or recommendation sets.

## Working Idea

Provisional substance under development before acceptance.

**Constraint**: Structure and chain steps remain outside the candidate until the person adopts them.

## Converged Proposal

The singular complete candidate governed by existing Profile convergence and acceptance rules.

**Constraint**: This feature does not change its shape, lifecycle, or acceptance boundary.
