# Data Model: Final Objectives Cleanup

## Business Objective evidence

Accepted evidence supporting the retained Objective Statement.

## Success evidence

Accepted evidence supporting at least one retained Success Measure.

## Highway Relevance evidence

Accepted downstream context that can improve later Highway use of the Objective.

**Persistence**: Not stored as a record field.

**Readiness**: Does not independently block review or creation.

## Synthesized Rationale

The retained explanation stored under `## Rationale` and presented as `**Why it matters:**`.

**Sources**:

- accepted Business Objective evidence
- accepted Success evidence
- accepted Highway Relevance evidence
- applicable accepted Profile evidence

**Exclusions**: Highway Identity, Highway Vision, and Highway Platform Objectives may frame Highway behavior but are not organizational facts for Rationale.

**Rule**: Use the concise rationale supported by accepted evidence; do not ask a separate Rationale question.

## Profile result

A Profile-owned result that may be usable, unavailable, or Profile-owned Blocked.

**Blocked behavior**: `A Profile-owned Blocked result blocks Objective behavior that depends on accepted Profile evidence.`

**Unavailable behavior**: An unavailable Profile remains distinct from a Profile-owned Blocked result.

## Selected recommendation

A displayed Objective recommendation whose selection is acceptance under the Experience Standard.

**Creation content**: Statement, Success Measures, and synthesized Rationale only from the recommendation and its grounding evidence.

**Missing Success**: Ask only for unresolved Success information.

**No redundant confirmation**: Do not ask for confirmation or a separate Rationale question.
