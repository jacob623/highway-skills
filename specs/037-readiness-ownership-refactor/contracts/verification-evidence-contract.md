# Verification Evidence Contract

Final verification reports these evidence classes separately:

1. **Executable owner behavior**: owner fixtures and status classification.
2. **Setup routing behavior**: ordered calls, short-circuiting, dashboard output, and zero-NFR completion.
3. **Static contract checks**: field order, allowed statuses, required skill documentation, and no duplicated owner rules in Setup.
4. **Generated-artifact checks**: catalog, adapters, distribution manifest, and clean regeneration.
5. **Limitations**: fixture or platform coverage that was not executable and any remaining validation gap.

Every owner fixture records the response, before/after artifact hashes, and repeated-run comparison. A claimed passing result must identify its command and result separately from requirement coverage.
