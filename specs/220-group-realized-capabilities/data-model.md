# Data Model: Group Realized Capabilities

## New realized capability

- Title: one noun phrase, five words or fewer, naming the automation. A hyphenated word counts as one word. No product name.
- Realized By: the platform.
- Evidence: each documented feature that performs the automation, and the handoff, such as a pull request, a comment, or a ticket.
- Proposed: only when the implementation's Realized Capabilities do not already mean that automation.

## Grouping

- Same automation, by meaning: one capability.
- Different work: separate capabilities. A scheduled or event-triggered run, a scripted run, proposing a change, reviewing a change, and a scan that is not a review of one change.
- One feature covering two automations: two capabilities, with the feature in both evidence lines.

## Order in `current`

Capture (steps 1 to 3), group (4), title and evidence (5), mark new (6), drop what the documentation no longer describes (7), unreadable documentation (8), go to the proposal (9).
