# Contract: Onboarding Capabilities

## overview

The instruction says to emit "Let's get to know your technology landscape" as a level-3 heading. The template lines start:

```text
Let's get to know your technology landscape

We'll connect existing capabilities so future recommendations align with [Name]'s target direction.

During onboarding we'll:

- **Identify the technology capabilities** that support your business.
```

The remaining bullets and lines stay as written.

## capabilities

After "Do not ask the person to invent the list.", the step states, in order:

1. A technology capability is something software, a platform, a service, or infrastructure does for the organization.
2. For each business activity that accepted context names, propose the technology capability that activity relies on, including when context names no system.
3. Title it for what the technology does, such as Online Payments, not for the business function, such as Sales. The title is not a product or vendor.
4. Leave out an activity that no technology supports. When two activities rely on one technology, propose one capability.

The item description placeholder is `[What the technology does for the organization.]`. "Do not include a provider." stays.

## map

The capability description placeholder is `[What the technology does for the organization.]`. Business Purpose stays.

## Verification

- The overview opens with the level-3 heading "Let's get to know your technology landscape".
- Each proposed capability names what a technology does for the organization. An activity that no technology supports is not proposed. An activity with no named system still yields its technology capability, and `map` asks for the provider.

## Rejected anywhere in the skill

- `What we'll do together`
- `This is where Highway helps you navigate your technology landscape.`
- `Identify important business capabilities`
- `[What the organization does.]`
