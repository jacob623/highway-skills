# Data Model: Onboarding Technology Capabilities

## Technology capability

- **Title**: What the technology does, such as Online Payments. It names no product or vendor.
- **Description**: What the technology does for the organization.
- **Grounding**: A business activity or a system that accepted context names.
- **Provider**: Not shown on the starting point. `map` shows the observed platform or `Unknown`, as today.

Rules:

- One capability per item. Two activities that rely on one technology yield one capability.
- An activity that no technology supports yields none.
- A capability the person adds or corrects keeps their wording, including a business function.
- AI-Assisted Engineering and Planning keeps its existing rule.

No state is written. The capability queue stays in memory, as today.
