This contract describes static source-document and generated-correspondence properties. It does not claim to prove live conversational behavior.

## Source Guidance

The authoritative Profile source must:

- retain the Identity, Vision, Competitive Path, and Guiding Principles Discovery Contribution Opportunities;
- remove the standalone Advisory question scaffolding, Contribution in practice, Possibility-list Contribution Opportunities, Structure-revealing contributions, and Organizational expression sections;
- retain the existing Interpretation confidence guidance;
- include the required structure-revealing sentence in all four Discovery Contribution Opportunity sections;
- preserve the shared reaction, convergence, acceptance, persistence, readiness, ownership, attribution, and retained-schema boundaries.

## Verification Guidance

The authoritative Profile verification guidance and directly corresponding static contracts must not retain:

- the worked-exemplar verification claim;
- the counter-example verification claim;
- the per-contribution acknowledgment claim;
- the acknowledgment-beyond-restatement claim.

## Generated Correspondence
