## Summary

Make `highway-objectives` a synthesis-first owner. The source skill will explicitly define the
Objective Development Lifecycle, proposal-first discovery exception, proposed Success Measures,
queue-only recommendation selection, one-review capture path, and an illustrative multi-selection
queue example. The four generated identity-copy adapters will be regenerated from the source, and
static contract checks will verify the new guidance while preserving existing ownership, mutation,
readiness, persistence, and Repository Context contracts.
