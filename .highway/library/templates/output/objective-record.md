## File Frontmatter

```yaml
---
id: OBJXXXXXX
title: "<derived title approved by the user>"
status: active
capabilities: []
---
```

## Body

```markdown
## Statement

<user-provided objective statement>

## Success Measures

<user-provided measurable success criteria>

## Rationale

<user-approved rationale>
```

The placeholders represent user-owned values. The template governs the presence and ordering of
record structure, including the reserved capabilities relationship field, not the meaning or
quality of those values.
